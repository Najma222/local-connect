You are helping me finish and polish "Local Connect", a hyperlocal marketplace for the FirstCommit '26 hackathon (CampusCode). I am a solo builder on an iPhone. Read README.md, docs/PRD.md and docs/TECH_STACK.md first.

PRODUCT: Customers browse registered local shops (bakers, crochet and gift makers, kirana stores, plumbers, electricians, garages, taxis and autos, student helpers), open a shop, add items to a one-shop cart, place an order and track it (placed, accepted, ready, completed). They can see shops on a live map, post a quick need (3 steps, optional voice to text) that matching shops answer with a price, and rate completed orders. Shop owners get a dashboard (orders, items with photo and optional AI autofill, requests). Admins verify shops. Users can switch between buyer and seller mode in Profile.

STACK: Next.js 14 App Router (JavaScript), Tailwind, Supabase (Auth, Postgres with RLS, Storage, Realtime), Leaflet, Vercel, PWA.
